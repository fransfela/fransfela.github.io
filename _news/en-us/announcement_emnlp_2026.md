🎉 Our paper **"Perceptually Better, Semantically Worse: Measuring Speech Enhancement Impact on LLM-Based Voice Systems"** (with Pejman Mowlaee) was accepted at the **EMNLP 2026 Industry Track**.

Speech enhancement improves perceptual metrics such as PESQ and STOI, but can change what a downstream LLM understands. We introduce the **Output Divergence Rate (ODR)** and show, for example, that MetricGAN+ more than doubles ODR (0.318 vs. 0.135) despite improving PESQ.

📦 Code and evaluation pipeline: [se-llm-odr-benchmark](https://github.com/fransfela/se-llm-odr-benchmark)
