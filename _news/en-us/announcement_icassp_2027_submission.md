📝 We submitted a paper on **conformal uncertainty for no-reference MOS prediction under distribution shift** to **ICASSP 2027**. It is currently under review.

The study asks whether feature-space distance can tell when a no-reference quality predictor (DNSMOS, NISQA, UTMOS) should not be trusted. Short answer: it flags domain shifts, but not individual wrong predictions.
